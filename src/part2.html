ConcernGuidance
NumeralsUse tabular figures for clocks, speed, range, temperature so values don't jitter (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums / fontFeatureSettings="tnum").
LocalizationFallback to Noto for CJK, Arabic, Hebrew, Devanagari, Thai. Allow 40% text expansion (German) and mirror layout for RTL.
ScalingUse sp. Support system font scale 1.0–1.3; layouts must reflow, not clip. Safety text is never scaled below 24 sp.
RenderingDisable synthetic bold/italic; avoid italics and all-caps beyond short labels (≤ 12 characters).
LicensingRoboto and Roboto Mono: Apache 2.0.
